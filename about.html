About

I am Marijn Chaouch, a photographer and filmmaker based in Amsterdam. I build stills and motion inside one concept, so a campaign holds together across every frame instead of falling apart between the photo and the film.

See the work

Trained at Fotoacademie Amsterdam, with a Moroccan and Dutch background that keeps showing up in how I look at light and people. The work is cinematic and driven by story, from quiet portraits to campaigns that need to move. I also fly drone, which opens the scale of a location without losing the intimacy of the ground.
